Who this is for

Built for Access students — and people wondering whether they need Access at all.

I originally collected these resources while preparing for an Access to HE Health course. The list grew into something broader: a practical study hub for mature and returning students who want to rebuild academic confidence and refresh health-related science.

FAQ

A few useful things to know.

Is this Access to HE Health study website really free?

Yes. There is no charge, advertising, mailing list or sign-up on this website. The linked materials were free to access when reviewed. Some external services may offer paid upgrades or ask you to make a free account, but you do not need to pay to use this study hub.

Which Access to HE courses is this useful for?

It is designed mainly around Access to HE Diplomas in Health, Health Science and Health Professions, and is especially relevant to students planning Nursing, Midwifery or other healthcare degrees. Exact modules vary by college and awarding body.

Do I need to complete every resource?

No. Definitely not. Use the list as a menu. Academic writing, study skills and evidence-based practice are broadly useful; biology, psychology, maths and other topics should be chosen according to your own needs and course content.

Can I use this before my Access course starts?

Yes. That was the original idea. It is particularly useful for mature students or anyone returning to education after a long break who wants to build confidence before classes begin.

Is this an official Access to HE course?

No. It is an independently curated collection of free study resources. It does not replace teaching, assessed work or the qualification itself, and it should not be used as a source of formal entry-requirement advice.
